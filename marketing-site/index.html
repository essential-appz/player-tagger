The problem

Most clubs are losing insight every weekend.

📷

Video without analysis

Your games are filmed but the footage just sits there. The moments that matter — the turnovers, the missed kickouts, the goals conceded — are buried in 70 minutes of raw video.

🧠

Relying on memory

Managers are making decisions based on what they remember from the sideline. That's not analysis — that's guesswork. Patterns stay invisible without data.

🔒

No dedicated analyst

County boards have analysts. Most clubs don't. That gap is growing. Match Tagger closes it — without hiring anyone, buying software, or learning a new tool.

🎯

Player development stalls

Telling a player what they did wrong is far less effective than showing them. Clipped video evidence changes the conversation in the dressing room.
